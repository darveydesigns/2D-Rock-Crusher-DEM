About the Project

This project is a Python-based DEM simulation that models the behaviour of rock particles inside a crushing chamber. The goal is to investigate particle flow, contact forces, breakage behaviour, and crusher efficiency while providing a lightweight educational and research platform.

Key Features

Particle Dynamics

Simulates individual particles with contact detection and force calculations.

Crusher Geometry

Models a simplified rock crusher with moving and fixed crushing surfaces.

Visualisation

Real-time rendering of particle motion, collisions, and crushing events.

Research Platform

Designed as an expandable framework for DEM and comminution studies.
